Spiral Grid — Scroll-Driven Zoom

Scroll down to fall into the spiral's eye. The whole layout is rotated and scaled on a single animation-timeline: view() keyframe, with transform-origin anchored at the convergence point.

Because every cell shares that convergence point as its own transform origin, each full scale(1/φ) rotate(-90°) step composes perfectly with the cells' own transforms — cell n slides into the exact position and orientation that cell n − 1 used to occupy. The spiral looks infinite even though there are only eight cells in the markup.

One full turn is four 90° steps. The animation runs over the scroll distance of the dark section below. Text rotates with the spiral during motion and lands upright at both ends (0° and −360°).

Design notes: doc/scroll-zoom.md. This prototype uses 8 cells, --fib-spiral-gap: 3px (kept small because the gap scales visually with the zoom — see the design doc), and a 4-step zoom (scale ≈ 6.854).

End of scene

At the end of the animation, cell 5 sits exactly where cell 1 started; cell 6 where cell 2 was; cell 7 where cell 3 was; cell 8 where cell 4 was. The layout is mathematically indistinguishable from the one you saw at the top — just shifted four levels in.

This is the move that powers the v2 infinite-zoom navigator. Same transform math, different input: scroll here, clicks or arrow keys there.

If scroll-driven animations aren't supported (or prefers-reduced-motion: reduce is set), the spiral falls back to a static render and this page reads as a normal article — the motion is the affordance, not the content.

Things to look for

  • The eye of the spiral stays locked to the viewport center for the entire zoom. That's the translate(−22.36%, −22.36%) + transform-origin trick: the convergence point is invariant under the scale/rotate, and the translate re-centers it in screen space.
  • Each quarter of the scroll range advances the spiral by one 90° step. Watch cell 2 slide into cell 1's slot during the first quarter, cell 3 into cell 2's slot during the second, and so on.
  • The --fib-spiral-gap clip-path survives the zoom and scales proportionally with it. Pre-zoom gaps of 3px end at ~20px at full zoom — expected behavior for a vector-scale-style zoom.
  • The fill cell (cell 8, golden rectangle) stays correct through every intermediate state because its own transform-origin is the same convergence point.
