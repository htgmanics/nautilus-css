Infinite zoom — the tunnel

Four full-size spirals stacked, layer k scaled by φ⁴ᵏ about the eye, so each one sits exactly in the previous one's fill cell. A step zooms the stack straight out of the eye by scale(φ⁻⁴) — no rotation; cells are born at the eye and fly past you. When it lands, layer 1 occupies exactly layer 0's box, so we re-render with offset+4 and drop the transform in the same frame — recycling the off-screen layer's DOM rather than rebuilding, so media in the other three layers keeps its decoded state. Wheel = continuous, snaps to the nearest lap when idle.
